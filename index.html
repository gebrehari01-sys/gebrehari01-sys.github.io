Sheet 01 · Causal ML

upliftpolicy

Picks customers to contact by how much a contact changes their decision, not by how likely they are to leave. It also flags the customers a contact would drive away.

  • Top 30% by uplift adds 4.47 points of retention; top 30% by churn score loses 0.23 points, on simulated customers.
  • Sleeping dogs make up 37% of the churn-ranked contact list but 4% of the uplift-ranked one.
  • Python
  • Uplift modeling
  • T-learner
  • Doubly-robust estimation

SourceLive demo

Sheet 02 · Document AI

fieldextract

Sends each extracted field to automatic approval or human review, with the cutoff set by the cost of an error, so operations can see which fields need a person.

  • Aiming for a 1% field-error rate, it auto-approves 43.8% of fields, with a realised error of 0.87% on held-out data.
  • Per-field routing costs 1.78x less than one tuned global threshold on the same 4,817 reviews.
  • Python
  • Isotonic calibration
  • Cost-based routing
  • PII redaction

SourceLive demo

Sheet 03 · Forecasting

hierforecast

Energy demand forecasts that add up: regional and sub-regional forecasts sum exactly to the national figure, and the upper tail is scored on its own.

  • On the p95 pinball loss, reconciled forecasts score 14.3% lower than bottom-up.
  • Independent forecasts disagreed by up to 612 MW; after reconciliation the gap is round-off.
  • Python
  • Reconciliation
  • Quantile forecasting
  • Pinball loss

SourceLive demo

Sheet 04 · LLM security

injectbench

Measures how often prompt-injection attacks get through, class by class, and which guardrail layers actually reduce that rate.

  • Attacks get through 10.8% of the time with the full guardrail stack, against 87.6% with none.
  • Roleplay framing is the weakest class at 46.5%, a figure a single overall rate would hide.
  • Python
  • Ablation study
  • Guardrails
  • Prompt injection
